Virtual DMM - Help

Practice using a Digital Multimeter (DMM) on aircraft electrical components. Select the correct function, place probes on test points, and interpret readings.

  • Drag probes: Click and drag the red or black probe tip from the DMM to a test point on the component. The probe will snap to the nearest test point.
  • Function select: Click the function buttons (DC V, AC V, etc.) to change what the meter measures.
  • Submit: Once you have your reading, click "Submit Reading" to check your answer.
  • Reset probe: Double-click a probe on the canvas to return it to the DMM.

DMM Functions

  • DC V - Measure DC voltage (batteries, bus bars)
  • AC V - Measure AC voltage (alternators, inverters)
  • Ω - Measure resistance (wires, components)
  • CONT - Continuity test (beeps if <50Ω)
  • DC A - Measure DC current (in-series only)
  • DIODE - Test diodes (forward voltage drop)

Safety

Always de-energize circuits before measuring resistance or continuity. Selecting ohms or continuity on a live circuit will trigger a safety warning.

Scoring

Each challenge scores on four criteria: correct function (25%), correct probe placement (25%), correct reading interpretation (25%), and safety compliance (25%).
