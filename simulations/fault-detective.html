Fault Detective - Help

You are an avionics technician on the flight line. An aircraft is AOG with a reported squawk. Your job is to systematically diagnose the electrical fault.

Circuit Under Test

28V DC Bus → Circuit Breaker → Toggle Switch → Wire → Wing Root Plug → Right Nav Light → Ground Stud

Phase 1: Initial Assessment

Choose the best starting point for your troubleshooting approach. Systematic methodology matters for your score.

Phase 2: Testing

  • Click highlighted test points on the circuit schematic
  • Select the correct meter function (DC V, Ω, or Cont)
  • Red and black probes are placed at the selected test point and circuit ground
  • Each measurement costs 1 step (affects Efficiency score)
  • Safety: De-energize the circuit before measuring resistance or continuity!

Phase 3: Diagnosis

Identify the fault type, location, and recommended corrective action.

Scoring

  • Accuracy (40%) - Did you correctly identify the fault?
  • Efficiency (30%) - Fewer measurements = better score (optimal: 3-4)
  • Safety (20%) - Did you follow safe measurement practices?
  • Methodology (10%) - Did you use a systematic approach?

Each replay presents a randomly selected fault from a pool of 6 scenarios. Successfully diagnosed faults are tracked.
