Crimp Lab - Help

Practice wire crimping procedures for aircraft avionics connectors. Follow each step carefully - crimping quality is critical to flight safety.

Procedure

  • Select Terminal: Choose the correct terminal type and gauge from the tray.
  • Strip Wire: Select the wire stripper and click the wire to strip it to the correct length.
  • Insert Wire: Click to slide the stripped wire into the terminal barrel.
  • Crimp: Select the ratcheting crimp tool (NOT pliers!) and click to crimp.
  • Inspect: Use Zoom, Pull Test, and Cross-Section views to evaluate the crimp.
  • Verdict: Click PASS or REJECT based on your inspection findings.

Tools

  • Wire Stripper - Removes insulation from wire end
  • Ratcheting Crimp Tool - Correct tool for aviation terminal crimps
  • Generic Pliers - INCORRECT for aviation crimps (inconsistent deformation)
  • Heat Gun - For heat-shrink tubing application
  • Inspection Mirror - For visual inspection of hard-to-see areas
  • Pin Insert Tool - For inserting/extracting pins in connectors

Levels

  • Level 1: Single ring terminal crimp (guided, with prompts)
  • Level 2: D-sub pin contact on shielded wire (guided practice)
  • Level 3: Full 6-wire harness termination (independent)

Inspection Criteria

  • Pull Test: Good crimp = 15-25 lbs PASS; Under-crimp = 3-8 lbs FAIL; Over-crimp = barrel cracks FAIL
  • Cross-Section: Wire fill should be 70-90% of barrel area
  • Visual: No cracks, no exposed conductor before barrel, no insulation in barrel
